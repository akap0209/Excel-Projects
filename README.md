# Excel-Projects
## US Government analysis of their public and governemental debt
By: Alex Kaplan 
## Data Source
The dataset has been extracted from US Government website. It shows information about debt held by the public, intragovernmental holdings, and total US outstanding debt.
## Data Contents
This dataset contains information on years of debt in the US.
## Data Cleaning Procedures
Data was arraned in tabular form and duplicates were removed. 
## Analysis Objective
This analysis aims to identify the years and months with the highest debt and forecast the debt levels for the upcoming two years.
## Research Questions
1. What was the Yearly Debt Percentage Increase for each year compared to the previous year?
2. Which months historically have seen the highest/lowest increases in Total debt?
3. What is the projected growth of the publicly held debt in the next few years?
## Data Limitations
There are also only 24000 rows of data which may not be enough of a sample to predict debt. We also do not know specifically what city or state a patient lives in.
## Data Ethics
The data was collected with the consent of its participants and none of their private information was included. Therefore, there are no ethical concerns with the data.
