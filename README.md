# 📘 U.S. Debt Tracker — Excel Data Analysis Project  
**By: Alex Kaplan**

## 📌 Project Overview  
This project analyzes historical trends in **U.S. public debt** using Excel. The goal was to understand how the national debt has changed over time, identify periods of rapid growth, and visualize long‑term patterns using PivotTables, formulas, and interactive dashboards.

The project demonstrates strong skills in **data cleaning, time‑series analysis, Excel automation, and dashboard design**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives  
- Track changes in U.S. public debt over time  
- Calculate month‑over‑month and year‑over‑year growth  
- Identify major spikes and economic events that influenced debt levels  
- Build an interactive Excel dashboard for exploring trends  
- Translate raw financial data into clear insights for decision‑making  

---

## 🧹 Data Preparation  
To prepare the dataset for analysis, I:

- Cleaned and standardized date formats  
- Removed duplicates and corrected formatting inconsistencies  
- Converted debt values into numeric format for calculations  
- Created structured tables to support PivotTables and charts  

This ensured the dataset was accurate, consistent, and ready for time‑series analysis.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Techniques Used  
- **PivotTables** for summarizing debt by year, month, and category  
- **Calculated fields** for MoM and YoY percentage change  
- **Line and bar charts** to visualize long‑term trends  
- **Slicers** to filter by time period  
- **Conditional formatting** to highlight spikes and anomalies  
- **Trendlines & projections** to estimate future debt levels  

---

## 📈 Key Insights & Results  

### **1. Long‑Term Growth Trend**  
- U.S. public debt shows a **consistent upward trajectory** across the entire dataset.  
- The long‑term trendline indicates sustained growth driven by federal spending, economic cycles, and policy decisions.

### **2. Major Spikes During Economic Events**  
- Year‑over‑year calculations revealed **significant increases during recession periods** and major federal stimulus efforts.  
- These spikes align with known economic downturns and recovery programs.

### **3. Seasonal & Monthly Patterns**  
- Certain months showed sharper increases, reflecting **fiscal cycles, budget approvals, and government spending schedules**.

### **4. Future Projections**  
- Based on historical patterns, projections suggest that **debt levels will continue rising** unless major policy changes occur.

### **5. Dashboard Insights**  
- The interactive dashboard makes it easy to explore:  
  - Debt growth by year  
  - Monthly fluctuations  
  - Percentage changes  
  - Long‑term projections
